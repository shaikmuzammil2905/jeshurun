Joint Development Agreement (JDA)

The __ : __ JDA Catalogue Structure

Agreement Concept

  • A Joint Development Agreement (JDA) divides land value and construction costs to give both parties equal returns (__:__).
  • The catalogue for this ratio typically details area sharing (splitting the built-up flats/villas), baseline specifications, and the premium timeline that the developer must provide in exchange for the land.

The Division of Space

  • Residential Apartments: The developer designs a multi-story or gated community. The flats are divided equally (e.g., the builder takes floors 1 & 2, the owner takes floors 3 & 4), complete with an Undivided Share of Land (UDS).
  • Parking & Common Amenities: Basement and stilt parking slots are distributed in the exact __ : __ ratio.
  • Terrace Rights: Often split equally.

Initial Soil & Site Preparation

  • Earth excavation.
  • Soil investigation & testing by an NABL-certified lab.
  • Site survey before the marking: an architect surveyor studies levels, identifies site reference coordinates and marks grids.
  • Pillar and column marking specifically identifies the exact placement of structural supports based on soil conditions and the foundation plan.

Core Specifications & Civil Works

Building standard benchmarks for premium durability

RCC Structure

  • RCC (Reinforced cement concrete ) structure design integrates high-compressive-strength steel reinforcement to create durable, load-bearing structures like footings, beams, columns, and slabs, as per design.
  • RCC staircase with 6” thickness soffit slab.
  • Lintels and chajjas for windows and doors.
  • JSW steel rods will be used as per structural design.
  • Ultra tech 53 grade cement use for slabs, columns, beams.
  • Birla 43 grade cement for brickwork and plastering.
  • River sand used for RCC works, brickwork and plastering.
  • Ceiling height 10’5” (slab to slab top).
  • Plinth beam maximum 2’ height from the natural ground level.
  • Coarse aggregate 12mm, 20mm, 40mm.
  • Sunken slabs for toilets.

Super Structure & Plastering

  • Red mud bricks in cement mortar use for the provide strong interlocking structural support for walls.
  • External walls will be 9” thickness.
  • Internal wall will be 4.5” thickness with 3 courses of concrete bed layers.
  • Install jail/mesh (chicken mesh) over the joint before plastering. The mesh should be about 200mm to 300 mm wide to adequately cover the joint area.
  • Both surfaces will be finished with plaster 1:6.
  • Double-coat external plaster will be 18 mm thick (1:6).
  • Double-coat internal plastering will be 12mm thickness (1:6 or 1:4).

Doors, Windows & Flooring

Premium fixtures and high-end surface finishes

Doors & Frames

  • Main door frame will be BT teak section size of 5” x 7” with teak wood panelled door/veneer shutter 36mm thickness as per the design or teak door 42mm.
  • Standard hardware: Godrej locking, hinges 5" long, 12” brass doorset.
  • Size of main door should be 5’ width and 7’ height including frame.
  • Internal door frame will be MT section size of 4” x 3” with FLUSH shutter of standard make.
  • Flush door will have a laminate finish (32mm).
  • Standard hardware: Godrej locking, SS hinges 5” long.
  • WPC frames and door 30mm for washrooms.
  • Bedroom doors and frames size 3’ width, 6’ 5” height including frame.
  • Washroom doors and frames size 3' width, 6’5” height including frame.

Windows, Ventilators & Flooring

  • All windows and ventilators will be 94 series UPVC Vaka, aluminium Domal or equivalent.
  • 3 tracker sliding windows with SS mosquito mesh.
  • Ventilators will be UPVC frame with glass louvres as per design, and size will be 16” x 20”. All ventilators will be fixed at the beam bottom.
  • Per Sft 750, glass thickness will be 4mm to 5mm.
  • All internal flooring will vitrified tiles 2’x2’ / 2’ x 4’ (kajaria or johnson). Per Sft 100/-.
  • Staircase finish with lapatra black or brown polish-finish granite. Per Sft 100/-.
  • Parking flooring with white rough granite. Per Sft 80/-.

Kitchen, Toilet & Plumbing Lines

Hygienic utility routing and top-tier sanitary installations

Kitchen & Toilet Setup

  • Elephant black granite platform with built-in sink (sink price 6000, tap price 2000).
  • Ceramic tile wall cladding up to 2 feet height on kitchen platform (per box 400/-).
  • Provision of municipal and bore well water along with provision for water purifier above kitchen counter.
  • Water supply pipes will be CPVC Ashirvad as required.
  • Internal drainage pipes PVC Sudhakar make, required diameter.
  • Toilet flooring with anti-skid ceramic tiles (1’ x 1 ’).
  • Wall ceramic tiles up to lintel level (1’ x 2 ’) (Per box 400/-).
  • Each portion separate water line.
  • Waterproofing – DR. Fixit or Fosroc – washroom, utility and terrace.

Drainage & Sanitaryware

  • Water supply pipes will be 1” CPVC (Ashirvad) of required diameter.
  • Internal drainage pipes will be Sudhakar or equivalent make PVC pipes of required diameter.
  • External drainage line (underground) will be foam core pipes Sudhakar or equivalent make and connected manholes as per requirement.
  • Internal drainage lines connect to main drainage line.
  • Rain water down-take 4” PVC pipes will be of Sudhakar pipes and soil pipes will be 6” pvc pipes.
  • Pipe internal plumbing of toilets in 1” CPVC pipes.
  • Brand hindware or parryware, cera. Per washroom: 50,000.
  • Ewc, seat cover & pvc cistern wall mounted.
  • Health faucet & 2-way bib cock.
  • Wash basin & pillar cock-with countertop.
  • Wall mixer & shower.
  • Angle cocks (4 nos.) – for geyser, kitchen, etc.
  • Bib cock with nozzle – for washing machine.

Finishes, Electrical & Services

Premium aesthetics, wiring configurations, and utility support

Railing, Painting & Polish

  • Glass Railing 10mm to 12mm toughened glass with SS supports (304).
  • MS sliding gate with panels as per design; lock arrangement will be provided.
  • MS safety grill for all windows.
  • One tap for Municipal water and one tap for bore well in the kitchen.
  • All Tapes are Parryware or Hindware, Cera make.
  • Wash basins for flat – Parryware or Hindware or Cera make.
  • Parryware, Hindware or Cera Commodes.
  • Interior: two coats of putty, one coat primer, two coats of PREMIUM EMULSION.
  • Exterior elevation side: one side texture or putty; coat the rest of 3 sides one coat primer, two coats WEATHER-PROOF exterior paints.
  • All door frames will be finished with melamine polish (natural colour matt finish) or enamel paint, any shade.
  • Main door will be finished with melamine polish (Natural colour matt finish).
  • Water supply system from Bore well through Syntex overhead tank (1000 litres) and municipal water supply with sump and a separate compartment in Syntex overhead tank (1000 litres).
  • Sump tank: 9” thickness brick wall masonry work with 10,000 litres and concrete slab.
  • Front-side compound wall will be as per design. The remaining three sides will be 4” thickness of standard height.

Electrical & Lift Operations

  • Concealed conduit wiring with copper cable of PolyCab, Finolex make, pipes Sudhakar made.
  • Modular switches of GM OR LEGRAND OR ANCHOR ROMA. Providing switches and sockets require location.
  • DB with MCB of Legrand make.
  • AC point in all bedrooms, hall, living room.
  • Geyser power point in all toilets.
  • Provision for power point exhaust fan in all toilets, chimney.
  • Provision for all bedrooms, living room, hall, TV power points.
  • Concealed P.V.C line provision with internet in hall only.
  • FAN point in all bedrooms, hall, living room, kitchen.
  • Facade lighting for elevation as per design.
  • Wires & conduit 1 sq.mm, 1.5 sq.mm, 2.5sq.mm, 4sq.mm.
  • Switches brand 50/- per switch.
  • Lift: Complete lift planning and installation solutions for residential, commercial, and apartment buildings. Smooth operation, advanced safety features, energy-efficient performance, and premium-quality finishes in compliance with industry standards.
